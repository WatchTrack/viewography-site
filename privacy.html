Privacy Policy.

Effective September 24, 2026

Viewography is designed to help you track and discover movies and television while keeping your viewing information under your control.

Information you add to Viewography

Viewography may store information you choose to create in the app, such as your watchlist, viewing history, episode progress, ratings, favorites, collections, preferences, and other app settings.

Local data and iCloud Recovery

Your primary Viewography library and viewing data are stored locally on your device. Viewography does not operate a server that receives or stores this information. If you choose to use Viewography's iCloud Recovery feature, a separate recovery copy may be stored in your personal iCloud account so you can restore your Viewography data. That recovery data is handled through your Apple account and Apple's iCloud services.

Movie, television, and availability information

Viewography uses third-party data services to retrieve movie and television metadata, streaming availability, and related program information. These services may receive technical information ordinarily associated with internet requests when the app communicates with them.

Tracking and advertising

Viewography does not sell your personal information. Viewography does not use your viewing history for third-party advertising or cross-app advertising tracking.

Data control

You can manage information within Viewography using the controls provided by the app. Features that connect to third-party services may also be subject to the data-management options offered by those services.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Viewography evolves. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

Questions about privacy or Viewography can be sent to support@viewography.app.
